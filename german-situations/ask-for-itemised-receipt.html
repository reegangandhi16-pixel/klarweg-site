B1 · Appointments and services

Request an itemised invoice in German

A repair invoice shows only a total. Ask for labour and parts to be listed separately.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Könnten Sie mir bitte eine Rechnung schicken, auf der Arbeitszeit und Ersatzteile getrennt aufgeführt sind?

Could you send me an invoice listing labour and replacement parts separately?

Auf der refers to the invoice. Getrennt aufgeführt specifies how the charges should appear.

Try it with a change

You receive a list with no dates. Ask when the repair took place and whether the date can be added to the invoice.
