Request written confirmation of a delay in German

You need written confirmation for an appointment you missed. Explain what happened and ask whom to contact.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Mein Zug ist mit vierzig Minuten Verspätung angekommen. Wo bekomme ich eine schriftliche Bestätigung?

My train arrived forty minutes late. Where can I get written confirmation?

Mit … Verspätung gives the amount of delay. Schriftlich distinguishes a document from a verbal answer.

Try it with a change

You need the confirmation by email. Ask whether it can include both the booked time and actual arrival time.
