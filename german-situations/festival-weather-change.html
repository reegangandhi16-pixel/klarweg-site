B1 · Reading and listening

Find which event changed due to weather in German

Read: Das Konzert im Park zieht in die Stadthalle um. Der Flohmarkt findet wie geplant draußen statt. Which event moved, and which did not?

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Das Konzert findet jetzt in der Stadthalle statt. Der Flohmarkt bleibt draußen.

The concert is now at the town hall. The flea market remains outdoors.

Umziehen means changing venue here. Wie geplant tells you the other event is unchanged.

Try it with a change

Suppose the flea market goes indoors too, but opens an hour later. Write two separate lines that state each change.
