B2 · Workplace

Flag a dependency before a deadline slips in German

You need data by Tuesday to send a report Friday. Explain the dependency and suggest what to do if the data arrives late.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Damit der Bericht am Freitag fertig wird, brauche ich die Daten bis Dienstag. Andernfalls würde ich zunächst eine vorläufige Fassung verschicken.

For the report to be ready Friday, I need the data by Tuesday. Otherwise, I would first send a provisional version.

Damit introduces the objective. Andernfalls connects the fallback to missing the Tuesday deadline.

Try it with a change

The data will arrive Wednesday morning. Give a revised schedule and say what you can prepare before the data arrives.
