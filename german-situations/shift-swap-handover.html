A2 · Workplace

Ask to swap shifts with a colleague in German

You have an appointment on Friday morning. Offer to take the colleague’s Saturday shift in exchange.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Kannst du am Freitagmorgen meine Schicht übernehmen? Dafür könnte ich am Samstag für dich arbeiten.

Can you take my Friday morning shift? In return, I could work for you on Saturday.

Dafür introduces the trade. Confirm any workplace approval separately.

Try it with a change

Your colleague can cover only the first half. Confirm the handover time and say who will ask the manager about the rest.
