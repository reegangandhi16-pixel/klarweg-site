Summarise both sides of a disagreement in German

One team values speed and the other wants more review. Summarise the trade-off without making either team sound unreasonable.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Während die eine Seite einen zügigen Abschluss anstrebt, legt die andere Wert auf eine gründlichere Prüfung. Offen ist, wie sich beide Ziele verbinden lassen.

While one side wants to finish quickly, the other values a more thorough review. How to combine these aims remains open.

Give each side an equally concrete goal. The last sentence states the decision still to be made.

Try it with a change

One side would accept a smaller release. Add this point as a possible compromise and keep both goals in your summary.
