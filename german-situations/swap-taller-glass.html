A1 · Daily life

Ask for a taller glass in German

At a café the glass is small. Ask for a larger one without complaining.

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Könnte ich bitte ein größeres Glas bekommen?

Could I have a larger glass, please?

Glas is neuter. Ein größeres Glas agrees with that noun in the request.

Try it with a change

Your tea is cold. Ask for another cup of tea using the same polite request pattern.
