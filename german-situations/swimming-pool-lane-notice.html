A1 · Reading and listening

Read a pool lane notice in German

Read: Bahn 3 ist heute von 14 bis 16 Uhr für einen Kurs reserviert. Can you use lane 3 at three? When does the reservation end?

Try your response

Say or write what you would use in this situation, then compare the wording.

Show the German model and English meaning

Nein. Um fünfzehn Uhr ist Bahn drei reserviert. Die Reservierung endet um sechzehn Uhr.

No. At three p.m. lane three is reserved. The reservation ends at four p.m.

Von … bis gives the time interval. Bahn means lane in this pool context.

Try it with a change

Replace 14–16 Uhr with 9–11 Uhr. Can you use lane three at half past ten? Answer in a full sentence.
